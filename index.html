ABOUT US

SIMBION FILM IS A FILMMAKING BOUTIQUE WITH A MULTIDISCIPLINARY APPROACH.

WE BELIEVE THAT THE FUNDAMENTALS OF FILMMAKING ARE TIMELESS. STORY, DIRECTION, COMPOSITION, LIGHT, MOVEMENT, AND PERFORMANCE REMAIN AT THE HEART OF EVERY MEANINGFUL VISUAL WORK.

BORN IN THE DIGITAL ERA, WE EMBRACE THE POSSIBILITIES OF NEW MEDIA WITHOUT LOSING SIGHT OF THE CRAFT.

FOR US, FILMMAKING IS NOT SIMPLY ABOUT PRODUCING CONTENT. IT IS ABOUT MAKING SOMETHING WORTH WATCHING.
